The house

An old Bang Rak house, restored for gathering

The house was built by the Portuguese, and before we arrived it was a café-bar. A Thai designer and Thai builders took it back to its bones and gave it a new life: a bar, a gallery hall, a main room for talks and music, and quiet rooms upstairs.

We kept what the street loved about it and made room for what comes next.

What we believe

Of the neighbourhoodWe work with our neighbours, not around them, so what a visit brings stays on the street.
