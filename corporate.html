Good to know

Can you handle dietary needs?

Yes. Tell us about allergies, halal, vegetarian or vegan needs when you book and we'll plan every meal around them.

What AV do you have?

A projector, a full sound system, microphones, modular lighting, whiteboards and chalkboards. WiFi covers both floors.

Can we visit first?

Please do. Walk-throughs are free and take about 30 minutes, any weekday.

Can we bring our own facilitator?

Of course. The house works just as well for your own programme, and our team will help set up the rooms.
