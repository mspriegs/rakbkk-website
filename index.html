History re-imagined

An old Bang Rak house, restored for gathering

This old heritage house in Bang Rak, Bangkok's district of love, was built by the Portuguese. A Thai designer and Thai builders took it back to its bones and restored it as a place to gather: a bar, a gallery hall, a main room for talks and music, and, for full-day buyouts, quiet rooms upstairs for smaller conversations.

Make a day of it

The house is only the beginning. Add an experience before or after your booking, led by the people who know this side of Bangkok best.

Food toursFamily kitchens and street-food favourites along Charoen Krung, starting with a bowl of noodles across the road.
Art walksGalleries, studios and the artists behind them, all a short stroll from our door.
River cruisesSee Bangkok from the Chao Phraya, from a sunset longtail ride to a private boat for the whole team.
Temple toursThe city's great temples with a guide who brings their stories to life.
Live musicBangkok's musicians in the room with you, from a string quartet at lunch to a DJ after dark.
Birthdays & celebrationsBirthdays, anniversaries and milestones. The house, the bar and the music are yours for the night.

Ask us to add any of these to your booking. We work with our neighbours, so the money your visit brings stays on the street.
